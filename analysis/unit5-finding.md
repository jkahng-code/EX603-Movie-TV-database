# Memo: Two Different Problems, Two Different Fixes

**To:** Leadership Team
**From:** Trust and Data Team
**Subject:** Recommended actions for movies with frequent spam and movies with no activity

Our last review identified the movies with the highest spam rates, but it
only covered movies that had received ratings. This time we connected
ratings to the full movie catalog, which revealed a second group: movies
that have never been rated at all. These two groups have very different
problems and need different responses.

**Group 1: Movies that fail often (movies 2 and 3)**

Movie 2 had 3 spam ratings out of 16 (18.8%), and movie 3 had 2 out of 13
(15.4%), about three times the platform rate of 6%. We checked whether
something about these movies explains it. Both are tagged Comedy, but the
other Comedy movies had little or no spam. Other movies from the same
countries also had little or no spam. Nothing in the movies themselves
explains the problem, which points to the rating activity around them.

*Recommended action:* Temporarily flag new ratings on movies 2 and 3 for
manual review, investigate the accounts that submitted the spam ratings,
and strengthen spam detection for these two movies. We do not recommend
removing the movies, because the problem appears to be suspicious rating
activity, not the content.

**Group 2: Movies that never started (movies 14 and 15)**

Movies 14 and 15 are in the catalog but have never received a single
rating. They were invisible in our earlier reports, because those reports
only counted movies that had ratings. Since they have no activity, there is
no spam problem here, and spam controls would do nothing.

*Recommended action:* First, confirm that both movies are available and
visible to users. If they are, improve their placement or promote them so
viewers have a chance to discover and rate them, then check back on whether
ratings start coming in.

**Also worth fixing: movie 13**

Movie 13 has ratings but has never been assigned a genre, so it won't
appear when users browse by category. Adding the right genres would improve
how it's organized and how easily people find it.

**What we know and what we don't**

The data confirms which movies have high spam rates and which have no
ratings. It does not tell us why. We don't know who is behind the spam on
movies 2 and 3, or whether movies 14 and 15 are hidden, newly added, or
simply unpopular. The recommended actions are designed to answer those
questions before any larger decisions are made.

**In short:** movies 2 and 3 need spam investigation and moderation, while
movies 14 and 15 need visibility and engagement. Treating them the same way
would fix neither.
